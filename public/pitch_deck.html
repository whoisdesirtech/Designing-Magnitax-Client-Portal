The Problem: Tax Prep is Broken

For Clients

  • Security Risks: Sharing sensitive SSNs and W-2 documents over unencrypted email or SMS.
  • Impersonal Service: Gig platforms assign a different anonymous freelancer every season.
  • Access Gaps: No central vault to view, search, or download historical tax filings.

For Preparers & Firms

  • Seasonal Bottlenecks: Extreme crunch in Jan-Apr, with zero recurring year-round revenue.
  • Outdated Workflows: Handling tax organizer documents via paper folders or clunky, legacy software.
  • No Ownership Path: Freelancers do the work while platform corporations pocket the equity.
Tax preparation is painful for clients and inefficient for firms. Clients are forced to send highly confidential info over unencrypted channels, while firms drown in paper organizers and lack recurring income. Modern users want security and continuity, which legacy tax chains simply fail to deliver.
